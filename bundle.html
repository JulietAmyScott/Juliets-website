Bundle to Bundle is an emerging startup founded by entrepreneurs who, as new parents, identified a gap in the retail market. As their son grew, the constant need for new clothes led to unexpected expenses and waste.


Bundle to Bundle offers a sustainable solution, bundled into one subscription clothing rental service.

When I began designing branding for Bundle to Bundle, to express “bundled solutions,” I created building blocks that symbolize the ease of packages arriving at your door. The soft color palette, while gentle, leans toward a more traditional, solid, and reliable aesthetic.
