me & design

design bio

As the informational canvas continues to evolve, I am fascinated with developing new ways to connect and engage people. I aspire to design with a purpose - to rouse conversation, inform, and establish meaningful relationships.


social media and small animal advocacy

I created the Smalls of San Pedro, an Instagram account dedicated to promoting Harbor Shelter's small animals. My responsibilities include photographing the animals, writing engaging adoption profiles, and advocating for improved small animal care by sharing educational content and resources.

I also launched a monthly adoption event and design the promotional materials to boost visibility and engagement.
